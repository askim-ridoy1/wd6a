The CREC4 Package WD6A of Dhaka Sanitation Improvement Project is a transformative infrastructure initiative located in the southeast of Dhaka, the capital of Bangladesh. This comprehensive project encompasses E1A and E1B Zones, covering a total construction area of approximately 5.76 square kilometers.

The project was awarded through a competitive bidding process and is being implemented with joint loan funds from the World Bank and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank. Our team is proud to be working in partnership with local Bangladeshi company ARIDOD to deliver this critical infrastructure.
